🏅 How Awards Work
Leading vs. Top Ceremonies
You'll notice both "Leading" and "Top" award ceremonies throughout the day. During Leading ceremonies, high-scoring entries are recognized and may be called back to a future Top ceremony where final Top 10 placements are announced.

Plan your day around the possibility of returning for additional ceremonies — you may be called back!

Competition Rules
📋 Adjudication
If a routine can't perform during its scheduled time, it may perform "for Adjudication" instead.

• Announced on stage at the end of the awards session
• Still receives the appropriate medal or trophy based on score
• Still qualifies for National Finals
• Receives judges' comments

Routines must perform before the awards session for that set to be eligible for overall awards.
🏅 Award Levels
Awards are based on predetermined point ranges — multiple routines in the same age group and category can receive the same award level.

🎭 Competition Levels
Competitive
Highest level. 5+ hours/week. Required for cash awards, Top Overall, and Crystal Award. Soloists at Competitive must stay Competitive for all solos, duet/trios, and groups at the same event.
Advanced
3–5 hours/week. Limited competitive experience. Soloists must stay Advanced for all solos. May participate in Competitive groups. No cash awards.
📐 Age Divisions
Ages are calculated as of January 1, 2026.

Solos: dancer's age on Jan 1.
Groups: average age of all dancers (round down). The group also cannot compete more than one division younger than its oldest dancer.
Overall Brackets
Mini · 8 & under Junior · 9–11 Teen · 12–14 Senior · 15–19 Adult · 20+
⏱️ Time Limits
Solo / Duet / Small Group · 3:00 Large Group / Super Group · 4:00 Production · 8:00
15% grace period allowed. Deduction: 0.1 pts/judge per 15 seconds over the limit.
🏆 America Loves to Dance — Friday Finals
Top 10 Competitive-level Small and Large Groups in each age division (Mini/Junior/Teen/Senior) are invited back Friday to compete again in front of the full judge panel, streamed live.

No dancer overlap allowed within the same age group. Announced Wednesday or Thursday at awards.

Advanced, Performance, and Shine level routines are not eligible. Production groups are also not eligible for America Loves to Dance (they compete Thursday in ProductionMania instead).
Cash Prizes
🥇 1st Place · $500 🥈 2nd Place · $250 🥉 3rd Place · $100
🎬 ProductionMania
All Production groups at Finals automatically compete in ProductionMania — a high-energy showcase held Thursday evening (the night before Friday Finals). All groups perform back-to-back with live hosting and their own award ceremony.

Cash prize: $500 + $500 gift certificate for Top Overall per age division.
📷 Filming & Photography
Videotaping and movie cameras are prohibited. Still photography is limited to photos of your own child. No tripods, monopods, or professional setups. Air horns are also prohibited in the audience.
🎵 Scoring Criteria
Judges evaluate five areas:

Costume Rhythm & Sound Routine Stage Presence Technique
Personalized written and audio feedback is provided for each entry. All judging decisions are final.
💡 Good to Know
• Routines must perform in scheduled order to be eligible for overall awards
• A rule violation not caught at a regional can still be applied at Finals
• Music must be uploaded in advance — cell phones and CDs not accepted
• Every routine receives a trophy; every dancer in a group receives a category medal
